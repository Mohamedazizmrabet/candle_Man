## Where to search for more
- **itch.io:** Game Assets → Free, then filter by the *Pixel Art* and *Top-Down* tags, or search "candle", "fire", "dark fantasy".
- **kenney.nl:** thousands of CC0 assets, mostly simple; good for UI sounds and placeholders.
- **opengameart.org:** filter by license (CC0) and read each file's own license.
- **freesound.org:** sound effects such as candle crackle, fire, wax drip, bell. Search, then filter the license to CC0.

## Notes for Wickfall
- **Nothing ready-made has candle golems or a wax world.** Use free packs for generic parts
  (dungeon floors, crates, fire VFX, UI frames, fonts, sounds). Make the unique parts yourself
  with the AI prompts: the player, enemies, boss, relics and the region looks.
- **Recolor everything to one palette.** Mixing art from 3 artists plus AI looks messy. In
  Aseprite, load your palette and convert each sheet to it.
- **Keep a credits list** (asset, author, link, license) in `Assets/_Wickfall/CREDITS.txt`,
  even for CC0 assets. You'll need it for the credits screen and store page.
